Setup and recovery

Refreshing sample data preserves demo passwords. Reset demo passwords only if you need new ones. Remove sample data deletes tagged fixtures; unrelated records and the six protected sign-in accounts remain. New items created manually during the walkthrough use their normal lifecycle controls.

Keep the Demo reminder check pending. Its reminder becomes due about two minutes after preparation; actual push delivery depends on maintenance, connectivity and device permissions. Sample forms simulate submission without sending or saving the fictional inputs.
