# Dashboard redesign

Mode: Operate. Scope: login, application shell, student day, management publishing, and shared component system.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A campus daybook that puts the next obligation on a readable line, with management operating in the same vocabulary. Remove the oversized metric-card scaffold and marketing copy from working screens.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light-gray writing surface, ink-blue navigation and headings, a restrained burnt-orange time accent, IBM Plex Sans, small square corners, clear rules, tabular dates and consistent outline SVG icons. No decorative gradients, floating statistic cards or icon mosaics.

STORY: A student sees what is due first, acts on it, then checks notices. A POC/CR sees the relevant workspace immediately, reviews current work and opens the editor deliberately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact identity rail and utility header; a concise page title; deadlines at the top of the main column; response requests and academic dates in a narrower adjacent column. Management begins with existing records and a labelled create action, with a contextual editor beside them when needed.

FORM: Campus daybook, chosen from timetable, registrar ledger, library catalogue, railway departure board, course reader, seminar programme and newsroom planning desk. The concept-seed launcher was invoked but its engine is unavailable; no seed assignment is claimed. Manual code-led execution follows the user's equal-priority brief.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Verification

One combined desktop/mobile inspection round, one corrective batch and one confirmation round. Preserve role workflows and verify navigation, editor disclosure, forms, response controls and accessibility with emulator/browser tests. Screenshots are synthetic browser captures, not generated illustrations.
